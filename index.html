🔎 What ideas do we agree with?
Which ideas seem convincing? What evidence or reasoning makes you agree with them?

Look at the original ideas and the responses. Did anyone add something that made an idea clearer, stronger or more complete?
⚡ What disagreements remain?
Are there ideas that your group still disagrees about? What reasons were given on each side?
💡 What new ideas or understanding emerged?
Is there something your group understands now that was not present in any of the original ideas?
